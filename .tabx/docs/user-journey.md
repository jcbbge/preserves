# User Journey: Peach Preserves

## Core Flow: Content Export

### 1. Arrival & Introduction
**User State:** Potentially anxious about preserving their personal content.

**Interface Needs:**
- Clear, welcoming introduction explaining purpose
- Visual design that connects to the Peach aesthetic
- Immediate clarity on what the app does and doesn't do
- Assurance about privacy and data handling

**Emotional Touchpoints:**
- Recognition of the value of their Peach content
- Trustworthiness indicators
- Connection to the Peach "safe haven" feeling

### 2. Authentication
**User State:** Cautious about providing credentials, eager to get started.

**Interface Needs:**
- Simple login form with clear indication of what's being asked
- Appropriate error messages for login issues
- Visual feedback during authentication

**Emotional Touchpoints:**
- Reassurance about credential security
- Clarity about how credentials are used
- Delightful transition animations to reduce anxiety

### 3. Connection Confirmation
**User State:** Seeking validation that their account is properly connected.

**Interface Needs:**
- Display most recent post as confirmation of successful connection
- Clear indication of what content is available for export
- Simple, prominent path to begin export

**Emotional Touchpoints:**
- Moment of recognition ("That's my content!")
- Excitement about preserving memories
- Building trust through transparency

### 4. Export Initiation
**User State:** Ready to begin, but may have uncertainty about what's involved.

**Interface Needs:**
- Clear "Download" button with supportive microcopy
- Information about what will be included in the export
- Optional: settings for customizing the export (if applicable)

**Emotional Touchpoints:**
- Setting appropriate expectations
- Empowerment through clear choices
- Building confidence in the process

### 5. Export Progress
**User State:** Potentially impatient, concerned about completion.

**Interface Needs:**
- Engaging, non-technical progress indicator
- Clear indication of what's happening (e.g., "Gathering your posts from 2020")
- Visual estimate of time remaining
- Option to pause and resume
- Explanation of what happens if they close the browser

**Emotional Touchpoints:**
- Entertaining or meaningful visualizations to reduce perception of wait time
- Celebration of milestones during the process
- Reassurance during network issues or delays

### 6. Export Completion
**User State:** Relieved, curious about what they've received.

**Interface Needs:**
- Clear confirmation of successful export
- Explanation of what's in the download and how to access it
- Information about what they can do with the exported content
- Option to start a new export if needed

**Emotional Touchpoints:**
- Celebration of successful preservation
- Nostalgia about preserved memories
- Sense of completion and safety

### 7. Help & Support (Throughout)
**User State:** May encounter confusion or technical issues at any point.

**Interface Needs:**
- Contextual help that anticipates common questions
- Clear paths to get support if needed
- Information about known limitations or issues

**Emotional Touchpoints:**
- Feeling supported rather than abandoned
- Confidence that problems can be solved
- Human connection despite the technical process

## Accessibility Considerations

Throughout all steps of this journey, the interface must:

- Work well with screen readers and assistive technology
- Support keyboard navigation
- Provide multiple ways to understand status (not just visual)
- Use clear, non-technical language
- Maintain sufficient color contrast
- Handle timeout and error states gracefully
- Allow users to recover from mistakes

## Technical vs. Emotional Balance

This journey must balance technical robustness with emotional sensitivity:

- Technical aspects should be handled "behind the curtain" when possible
- User-facing language should focus on the emotional value of the content, not the technical process
- Progress indicators should feel encouraging, not clinical
- Success states should celebrate the meaning of what's been preserved, not just the fact of successful data transfer
- Interfaces should mirror the care and attention users put into creating their content
